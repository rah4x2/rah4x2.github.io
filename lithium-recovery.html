01 / PROCESS ARCHITECTURE

A recovery train built around emerging separation technologies.

The project began with waste-battery black mass and developed an integrated process through leaching, impurity control, CDI, forward osmosis and post-treatment toward lithium carbonate production.

PROCESS_01Hover or tap a unitExplore the recovery train
BLACK MASS → LITHIUM CARBONATE
01

Leaching + pretreatment

Black mass is converted into an aqueous leachate and conditioned to support downstream lithium recovery.

02

CDI + FO

Capacitive deionization and forward osmosis were selected as emerging technologies for selective recovery and concentration.

03

Post-treatment

Crystallisation, filtration and drying complete the route to lithium carbonate product specification.

02 / ENGINEERING ANALYSIS

Design decisions were tested from several angles.

The project was not just a process sketch. It included balances, equipment decisions, simulation, plant layout and feasibility analysis.

MASS + ENERGY

Material & energy balances

Used to validate flows across the process, estimate product recovery and provide a basis for downstream sizing.

EQUIPMENT

Selection + sizing

Alternative process options were compared using technical decision criteria before the integrated design was refined.

03 / PROCESS SAFETY

Safety was treated as a design input, not a final checkbox.

HAZOP-style thinking was used to identify hazards, process impacts, environmental consequences and control measures across the recovery train.

HIGH TEMP / PRESSURE

Thermal runaway or overpressure → cooling systems, alarms and pressure relief.

GAS FORMATION

H₂ / CO₂ formation → ventilation and gas detection.

MEMBRANE FOULING

Reduced efficiency → pretreatment and cleaning strategy.

CORROSION / LEAKAGE

Equipment failure and contamination risk → compatible materials and containment.

04 / ECONOMICS

Could the process make engineering sense and financial sense?

The project included equipment costing and discounted / non-discounted profitability analysis to test industrial feasibility.
